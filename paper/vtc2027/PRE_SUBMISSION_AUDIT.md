# Full pre-submission audit

Audit date: 2026-10-03

This document records the issues found during a line-by-line scientific and editorial review of the VTC2027-Spring draft and the corrective action applied. It is not part of the submitted PDF.

## Material corrections

1. **Active-link semantics clarified.** The simulator activates `floor(N/2)` one-way disjoint links per snapshot, not `N` simultaneous transmitters. The abstract, system model, results, and conclusion now state this explicitly.
2. **Resource-grid claim narrowed.** The paper no longer implies a standards-complete PSSCH mapping. It states the explicit study profile: 2 control/PSCCH-like symbols, 1 guard symbol, 11 PSSCH symbols, and 24 DM-RS RE/PRB; PSFCH, SL-PRS, and detailed SCI accounting are excluded.
3. **5G-LENA evidence characterized correctly.** The BLER source is described as generic NR EESM link-level simulation data. The manuscript now discloses closest-CBS mapping, linear SINR interpolation, endpoint clamping, and independent-code-block TB-BLER reconstruction.
4. **Channel-model domain disclosed.** The A2A fit is tied to the Erdemir et al. 3.5 GHz/50 MHz/30 dBm/100 m campaign. The synthetic square can create links up to 1.41 km, so the longest simulated links extrapolate beyond the roughly 1 km A2A trajectory.
5. **Perfect resource orthogonality disclosed.** Abstract frequency resources do not interfere in the model; adjacent-channel/inter-carrier leakage is not modeled.
6. **Allocator made reproducible in the paper.** The geometric conflict-weight formula and greedy assignment rule are now stated.
7. **Short-link pairing renamed precisely.** The robustness topology is a greedy globally-shortest non-overlapping pairing, not a claim that every node independently chooses its nearest neighbor.
8. **Goodput definition made explicit.** Goodput is expected first-transmission PHY payload rate, `TBS*(1-TB-BLER)/0.5 ms`, under one scheduled TB per active link per slot; it is not application throughput.
9. **Operating-envelope interpretation narrowed.** Qualification uses scenario-mean point estimates, not lower confidence bounds. The envelope is an engineering-policy summary, not a reliability or capacity guarantee.
10. **Failure-composition figure removed.** The original figure averaged per-seed conditional fractions and was easy to misread as a pooled composition. The submission retains only a clearly labeled heuristic N=100 diagnostic.
11. **Non-monotonic resource result reworded.** The R=2 versus R=4 goodput difference is attributed to the joint effect of interference, allocation, and TBS reduction rather than bandwidth alone.
12. **Recent overlapping work added.** Yu and Jiang (VTC2025-Fall) is cited for dynamic MCS scheduling in U2U sidelink.
13. **UE bandwidth standard corrected.** The 50 MHz / 30 kHz / 133-RB profile is cited to TS 38.101-1 rather than the base-station specification TS 38.104.
14. **AI-use disclosure corrected.** The acknowledgment states editing/bibliographic assistance and explicitly says simulation data were not generated by AI.

## Remaining scientific limitations

- static equal-altitude synthetic geometry;
- large-scale path loss only, without correlated shadowing or fast fading;
- one-way full-load links rather than mission traffic/queues;
- simplified sidelink resource grid and non-normative conflict-graph scheduling;
- ideal inter-resource orthogonality;
- generic NR link-level BLER curves rather than PSSCH-specific measured/link-level validation;
- idealized instantaneous-SINR adaptive MCS;
- experimental directional desired/interferer advantage rather than a physical beamforming model;
- policy thresholds chosen by the study rather than 3GPP requirements.

These limitations are now stated in the manuscript and bound the interpretation of the numerical results.
