## Citation-use rule

External citations support background facts, standardization statements, simulator/channel provenance, and comparisons with prior work. The numerical results in the Results section are generated by this repository's audited simulations and therefore are not attributed to external publications.

The manuscript deliberately avoids citing a source merely because it is topically related. Each citation should support the sentence or clause immediately preceding it.
