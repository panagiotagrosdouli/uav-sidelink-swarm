## Citation-use rule

External citations support background facts, standardization statements, simulator/channel provenance, and comparisons with prior work. Numerical results in the Results section are generated by this repository's audited simulations and are not attributed to external publications.

Each bibliography entry must be used in the manuscript, and every citation key must resolve. The CI performs this check automatically.
